# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## AI-Powered Citizen Grievance Intelligence & Resolution Platform

**Project type:** Smart India Hackathon (SIH) / Techathon solution  
**Document status:** Submission-ready  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Primary outcome:** Make public grievance reporting accessible, actionable, transparent, and measurably faster to resolve.

## 1. Product overview

The AI-Powered Citizen Grievance Intelligence & Resolution Platform is a multilingual, omnichannel civic-service platform that enables citizens to report local public-service issues through text, voice, photographs, and location. Artificial intelligence assists—not replaces—public officials by classifying complaints, identifying duplicates and emerging incidents, estimating urgency, proposing accountable departments, and surfacing evidence required for closure.
